  WHO WE ARE

Building Homes That Think Ahead

NeoNest started with a simple idea: a home should adapt to you, not the other way around. Today we help thousands of households live smarter, safer, and more efficiently.

OUR STORY

From One Idea to 50,000+ Homes

NeoNest was founded to remove the friction from smart home technology. Instead of juggling five different apps for lights, locks, and cameras, we built one connected ecosystem that just works.

What started as a single smart plug has grown into a full lineup of hubs, sensors, lighting, and security devices — trusted by homeowners across Nigeria and beyond.

We're still driven by the same goal we started with: make every home a little smarter, a little safer, and a lot more comfortable.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Core Values

Every product we build is measured against the same four principles.

Innovation

We build for how people actually live, not just for what's technically possible.

Reliability

A smart home that fails when you need it most isn't smart. We test for the edge cases.

Simplicity

Setup should take minutes, not a manual. If it's confusing, we haven't finished the job.

Sustainability

Smarter energy use isn't just a feature — it's a responsibility we take seriously.
